# Community conduct

Treat contributors and users respectfully. Welcome questions and constructive disagreement. Do not harass, discriminate, disclose others' private information, or make personal attacks. Critique ideas and code using evidence.

Maintainers may remove abusive content and restrict participation after reviewing context. Maintainers must apply these rules fairly. A private reporting contact must be added by the project owner before opening community participation; do not publish sensitive reports in public issues.
